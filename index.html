License plate game FAQ

How do you play the license plate game?

Watch for license plates from other states, provinces or countries. In License Plate Hunt, start a trip, tap Spot a Plate! each time you see a new one, and end the trip when you arrive. The app keeps the list and adds up the rewards.

How do rewards work?

Pick a template. Proximity to Home pays more for plates that traveled farther from your home state. Flat Rate pays the same for every plate. Show rewards as money, points, stars or something custom.

Is License Plate Hunt free?

Yes. The iPhone app is free to download and supported by ads. The browser version for Android and other devices is free with no ads.

Is there an Android version?

Yes. It runs in your browser, so there is nothing to download from a store. Open the link in Chrome, then choose Add to Home screen to use it like an app.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Your trips and history are stored on your phone, not in the cloud.

Is there a printable license plate checklist?

Yes. Print the free checklist with all 50 states and Canada's provinces and territories. No email needed.
