License Plate Hunt

A road trip game. Spot plates from the US, Canada, Mexico & China!

Start a trip, tap each plate you spot, and watch the rewards add up. Plates from farther away can be worth more. When you end the trip, your earnings are saved to History. It works offline, in English and Chinese, and keeps your trips on your device.
